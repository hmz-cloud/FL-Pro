Our Story

Born From Real Operations.

Built For Real Work.

FleetOps Pro wasn’t created in a meeting room — it was born on real project sites. After years working with large contracting and project-driven companies, we saw the same problems repeating: fleets scattered across multiple locations, vehicles moving without records, drivers relying on calls and WhatsApp, and operations teams trying to manage millions in assets using spreadsheets and guesswork.

The issue was never effort — it was visibility. Transport managers, project directors, and operations teams across Saudi Arabia all shared the same frustrations: unclear accountability, missing transfer logs, delayed maintenance, and systems that were either too complicated to adopt or too limited to trust.

FleetOps Pro was built as the answer.
